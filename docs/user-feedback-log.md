# Kaya: User feedback log

## 2026-11-09 · Tester 1 (salaried employee, works in tech, "paycheck-to-paycheck")

**Who she is:** regular salaried employee, tech background, no investments yet. Not the core target (people with money in several countries), but she represents a big group: *"nagsisimula pa lang mag-ipon"*.

**Insights (Taglish, sa salita niya)**
1. **UI/UX: pasado.** "Very easy to use", malinis ang interface, okay ang colors, CTAs at navigation. Walang major usability issue.
2. **Hindi niya ma-relate ang "wealth".** "Wala akong wealth to manage, sahod and go lang ang peg." Ang salitang *wealth* at *net worth* ay pakiramdam niya ay para sa mayayaman, kaya feeling niya hindi para sa kanya ang app.
3. **Nalilito sa terms.** Hindi siya "wealth person", kaya ang ibang financial terms (hal. net worth, liquid vs locked, allocation, liabilities) kailangan pang i-Google. Gusto niya ng simpler words o maliit na tooltip/explanation.
4. **Kailangan ng walkthrough o demo.** First-time user siya, hindi niya alam kung ano ang tinitingnan niya at ano ang dapat gawin. Suggestion: quick walkthrough o sample/demo portfolio.
5. **Hindi agad malinaw ang "WHY".** Ano ang problemang sinosolve ng Kaya? Ano ang kaya nitong gawin na hindi kaya ng banking o investment apps niya?
6. **Para rin dapat sa nagsisimula.** Hindi lahat ng gagamit ay may malaking assets na. May chance gawing mas approachable ang experience para sa nagsisimula pa lang mag-ipon.
7. **Depende sa target.** Kung older at hindi masyadong tech-savvy, kailangan ng simpler terms. Kung younger at sanay sa fintech, okay na ang current terms.

**Her own caveat:** "Baka hindi talaga ako ang target market mo", pero useful pa rin ang perspective ng nasa labas ng target.

**Decisions / next steps:** see the recommendation in the session notes (stick with the strategy, broaden the framing, plain-language layer, demo mode, clearer "why").

## 2026-11-10 · Tester 2 (detailed UX review)

Overall: likes the clean design. Their top priorities: charts, balance wording, slide-up forms.

1. Overview chart shows a trend after one balance while the account chart is blank. Show "Add another balance to see your progress".
2. Exchange rates have no source or update time; foreign income conversion doesn't explain the rate used.
3. Liabilities sit under "New Asset". Offer "Add asset / Add debt".
4. "Add Balance" sounds like a deposit. Use "Update balance" + "Enter your total balance". Explain whether income changes the balance.
5. Bottom nav icons have no labels.
6. Portfolio card has empty space; dots don't signal the Liquidity view. Use "Allocation / Liquidity" tabs.
7. Slide-up forms: grab bar doesn't close on tap; add tap/swipe-down to dismiss. Some controls need screen-reader labels.
8. Premium card says "Unlock" but does nothing. (Already removed, replaced by Ko-fi tip jar.)
9. Income average with one entry is spread over several months. Explain which months count.
10. Signup form has no visible Privacy Policy / Terms links.
11. Faint small text in dark mode; "Clear all data" should look destructive.
12. "1 accounts" plural bug; "11/5/2026" should read "Updated 5 Nov 2026".
13. Landing "Start for free" doesn't explain free vs paid while Settings promoted Premium.
